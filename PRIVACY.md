RATATOSKR — PRIVACY POLICY
Last Updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the independent software developer operating under the GitHub pseudonym "Natsuru" ("Licensor", "we", "us") handles data when you use the "Ratatoskr" software product ("Software").

We are committed to data minimization. The Software is designed to run locally on your infrastructure and does not collect, harvest, or sell your personal data or application telemetry.

### 1. DATA WE COLLECT AUTOMATICALLY
When the Software operates in Commercial Mode, it may periodically connect to our licensing server solely to verify the validity of your commercial license key. During this connection, the server may process:
- License Key: The unique alphanumeric string purchased by the Licensee.
- IP Address: Automatically visible to the server during the network request (classified as personal data under GDPR).
- Hardware Identifier (HWID): A non-reversible cryptographic hash generated from your system parameters to prevent license sharing.
- Software Version: To ensure compatibility.
### 2. PURPOSE OF DATA PROCESSING
We process the information listed in Section 1 strictly for the following purposes:
- To prevent software piracy and unauthorized concurrent use of commercial licenses.
- To enforce the End-User License Agreement (EULA).
### 3. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING (GDPR COMPLIANCE)
If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), our legal basis for collecting and processing this data is Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR — processing is necessary for the performance of a contract (the EULA) to which you are a party.
### 4. DATA RETENTION
We do not store or track your IP address or HWID history continuously. Server logs containing IP addresses are automatically rotated and permanently deleted after thirty (30) days. Validated license keys remain stored as long as your subscription is active.
### 5. THIRD-PARTY DISCLOSURE
We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your data with any third parties. Financial transactions and billing data are handled entirely by our authorized Merchant of Record (MoR) platforms (e.g., Lemon Squeezy, Paddle, Gumroad). We do not have access to your credit card or payment details.
